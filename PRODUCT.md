# Product

## Register

product

## Users

League of Legends players who track drafts during champion select, with optional local synchronization and manual corrections. PickBoard is typically used under time pressure beside the game client, where users need to review picks and bans, record likely enemy positions, consult personal champion pools, and copy a structured prompt for an AI assistant.

## Product Purpose

PickBoard reduces the effort required to transform a partial or complete draft into context that an external assistant can understand. The product must remain useful without the League Client, preserve local state, follow only confirmed data when synchronization is available, and make corrections fast with mouse or keyboard. Success means opening the application, synchronizing or assembling a draft, and copying an accurate prompt within seconds.

## Brand Personality

Precise, focused, and dependable. The product voice is direct, with terminology familiar to League of Legends players and brief feedback. PickBoard should communicate the tactical clarity of a draft board without resembling the official game client.

## Anti-references

- Do not copy the client, visual identity, logos, or official Riot Games iconography.
- Do not resemble a generic SaaS dashboard, promotional landing page, or flashy streaming overlay.
- Avoid excessive cards, decorative glow, glassmorphism, slow animation, or unconventional controls when familiar patterns are sufficient.
- Do not sacrifice density, speed, or readability to simulate a fantasy aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. The draft is always the primary task and must be immediately visible.
2. Frequent actions must be fast, reversible, and keyboard-accessible.
3. State and restrictions should be understandable where each decision happens.
4. Density should serve the in-game context without compromising readability or precision.
5. The product remains useful and predictable without a network connection or the League Client.
6. Local integration is read-only and never infers enemy positions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target at least WCAG 2.1 AA on primary surfaces, including a 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio for regular text, visible focus indicators, accessible labels, complete keyboard navigation, and states that do not depend on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences, use no sound, and preserve appropriate interactive targets at 1366×768 and 1920×1080.
